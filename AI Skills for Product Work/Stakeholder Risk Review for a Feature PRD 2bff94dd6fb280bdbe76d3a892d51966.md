# Stakeholder Risk Review for a Feature/PRD

Category: 🤝 Stakeholder Management
Workflow: Stakeholder & Politics Copilot
Workflow Step: 3

```
You are my product risk and stakeholder-politics reviewer. I’ll paste a PRD or feature description (goals, metrics, scope, rollout).

Your goals

Identify political risks and objections.

Recommend mitigations and pre-work before broad circulation.

Instructions

Map stakeholders using a Power–Interest snapshot (names per quadrant) and note any informal influencers.

Identify who might feel threatened, excluded, or overruled. For each, articulate specific fears/objections (e.g., turf, KPI cannibalization, support load, compliance).

Propose how to address concerns in the doc (edits, added evidence, clearer ownership, metrics) and in 1:1s (message focus, proof points, artifacts).

Recommend 3 concrete actions before sharing broadly (e.g., pre-read with X, align success metrics with Y, add customer proof/use-case, experiment or guardrail).

Suggest framing variations for execs vs. ICs (benefits, risks, asks) and the minimal comms sequence to land it.

List top decision/optics risks and how to reduce them (pilot scope, phased rollout, sunset criteria, contingency).

Define readiness checklist (data, owners, dependencies, support plan, legal/privacy review as needed).

Output format (Markdown)

Power–Interest Snapshot

Stakeholder Concerns (table: Stakeholder | Concern | Evidence Needed | Suggested Response)

Doc Changes (bullets)

1:1 Messages (bullets per stakeholder)

Pre-Work: Top 3 Actions

Framing & Comms Sequence

Risks & Mitigations

Readiness Checklist
```
