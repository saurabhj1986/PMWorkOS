# Product Design Analyzer

Evaluate product designs and provide actionable improvement suggestions based on context and screenshots.

## Workflow

1. **Gather inputs** - Request or confirm:
   - Context about the product (goals, target users, success metrics)
   - Screenshots or images of the design to analyze

2. **Conduct analysis** across four dimensions:
   - **Issues & limitations**: Identify usability problems, visual inconsistencies, accessibility concerns
   - **Optimization opportunities**: Suggest improvements aligned with stated goals/metrics
   - **Alternative approaches**: Propose different UX patterns, layouts, or features
   - **Pros & cons**: Evaluate tradeoffs of the current design

3. **Deliver structured feedback** with:
   - Clear, specific observations (not vague critiques)
   - Reasoning tied to goals/metrics
   - Prioritized recommendations

## Output Format

Structure the analysis as:

```
## Issues & Limitations
[Specific problems with explanations]

## Optimization Opportunities
[Actionable improvements with rationale]

## Alternative Approaches
[Different solutions to consider]

## Pros & Cons
[Balanced evaluation of current design]
```

## Best Practices

- Ground feedback in the stated goals and metrics, not personal preference
- Be specific: "The CTA button has low contrast (3.2:1)" not "The button is hard to see"
- Consider the full user journey, not just individual screens
- Balance critique with recognition of what works well
