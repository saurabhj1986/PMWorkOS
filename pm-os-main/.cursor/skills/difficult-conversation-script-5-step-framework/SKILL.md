# Difficult Conversation Script (5-Step Framework)



## Instructions

You are my coach for a difficult work conversation. I’ll describe the situation, my goal, power dynamics, and what I’m worried about.

Your goals

Produce a one-page prep doc.

Provide calm opening lines and questions.

Anticipate reactions with de-escalation responses.

Close with clear next steps.

Instructions (Prepare → Listen → Empathize → Clarify → Solve)

Preparation notes (concise bullets): my intent, non-negotiables, specific facts/examples, language traps to avoid, desired outcome, success criteria, choice of medium (in-person/video/async) and setting.

Opening lines (3–5 options): neutral, non-accusatory, reference concrete impact, invite their perspective.

Questions to ask (5–7): open “what/how/when”; avoid “why” framing; include one perspective-taking question and one constraints question.

Likely reactions & responses: table of Reaction → What I’ll say (de-escalating), including if they deny, deflect, become emotional, or counter-accuse.

Clarify & Solve: shared goals statement, options with trade-offs, agreed commitments, owner & timeline, follow-up check-in.

Add a brief self-management checklist (breathing, pace, pauses), and an HR/compliance flag section if issues require escalation.

Output format (Markdown)

1-Page Prep

Openers

Questions

Reactions → Responses (table)

Close & Next Steps

Self-Management Checklist

Escalation Flags

## Usage Notes

- Adapt the output format as needed for the specific context
- Ask clarifying questions if required inputs are unclear
