## Frameworks/ (119 files across 4 stages)

Organized by PM lifecycle stage. Each framework includes metadata on actionability, risk focus, and type, plus a link to a case study or source.

### discovery/
Frameworks for discovering what to build. Includes: `opportunity-solution-tree`, `jobs-to-be-done`, `user-story-mapping`, `lean-canvas`, `design-sprint`, etc.

### validation/
Frameworks for validating what we're building. Includes: `a-b-test`, `usability-test`, `wizard-of-oz`, `concierge`, `fake-door-testing`, etc.

### build/
Frameworks for building and delivering. Includes: `scrum`, `kanban`, `shape-up`, `story-mapping`, `rice`, etc.

### grow/
Frameworks for growing and scaling. Includes: `north-star-metric`, `growth-loops`, `aarrr`, `heart`, etc.
