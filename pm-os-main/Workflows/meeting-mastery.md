# Workflow: Meeting Mastery

A 5-step workflow to run effective PM meetings that drive decisions and action.

## Overview

Use this workflow for important meetings where you need alignment, decisions, or progress—not for routine standups or 1:1s.

**Time:** 15-30 min prep, meeting duration, 10 min follow-up
**Output:** Decisions made, actions assigned, everyone aligned

---

## Step 1: Define the Meeting

**Goal:** Be crystal clear on why this meeting exists.

### Prompt

```
Help me define this meeting's purpose.

Meeting context:
- Topic: [What this meeting is about]
- Attendees: [Who will be there and their roles]
- Duration: [How long]
- Recurring: [One-time or regular]

Help me clarify:
1. **Purpose:** Is this to inform, discuss, or decide?
2. **Desired outcome:** What's true after the meeting that wasn't before?
3. **Required attendees:** Who MUST be there vs. optional?
4. **Pre-work:** What should people review/prepare beforehand?
5. **Success:** How will I know if this meeting was worth the time?

If the meeting doesn't have a clear outcome, should we cancel it?
```

### Output
- Clear meeting purpose
- Defined outcome
- Pre-work identified

---

## Step 2: Build the Agenda

**Goal:** Structure the meeting for maximum effectiveness.

### Prompt

```
Help me build an agenda for this meeting.

Meeting purpose:
[Paste from Step 1]

Duration: [X minutes]

Create an agenda that:
1. Starts with context/framing (max 20% of time)
2. Focuses most time on discussion or decision (60%)
3. Ends with clear next steps (20%)
4. Has time buffers for tangents
5. Assigns an owner to each section

Include:
- Time allocation per section
- Who leads each section
- What decision/output each section produces
- Explicit "parking lot" for tangents

Make sure the most important thing isn't last.
```

### Output
- Timed agenda
- Section owners
- Expected outputs per section

---

## Step 3: Prepare for Discussion

**Goal:** Anticipate dynamics and steer toward outcomes.

### Prompt

```
Help me prepare for the discussion dynamics.

Attendees:
[List attendees and their likely perspectives]

Key decision/topic:
[What we're discussing or deciding]

Help me anticipate:
1. **Different perspectives:** What will each person's position be?
2. **Potential conflicts:** Where might people disagree?
3. **Hidden agendas:** What are people not saying but thinking?
4. **Blockers:** What could derail the conversation?
5. **Alignment tactics:** How do I build consensus?

Also prepare:
- Questions to ask if discussion stalls
- How to handle a dominant voice
- How to handle silence
- Fallback if we can't reach decision

Give me 3 key questions to drive toward resolution.
```

### Output
- Stakeholder perspectives mapped
- Discussion facilitation plan
- Key questions prepared

---

## Step 4: Run the Meeting

**Checklist for during the meeting:**

### Opening (First 2 min)
- [ ] State the purpose: "We're here to [decide/discuss/align on] X"
- [ ] Confirm the outcome: "We'll leave with Y"
- [ ] Set ground rules if needed: "I'll keep us on time"

### During Discussion
- [ ] Keep a visible parking lot for tangents
- [ ] Check for quiet voices: "Sarah, what's your take?"
- [ ] Summarize periodically: "So we agree on X, but not Y"
- [ ] Call out when you're stuck: "We seem stuck—let me try a different framing"

### Closing (Last 5 min)
- [ ] Summarize decisions: "We decided X"
- [ ] Confirm actions: "Alex will do Y by Friday"
- [ ] Note open items: "We still need to figure out Z"
- [ ] Confirm next steps: "We'll meet again when..."

### If Things Go Wrong

| Problem | Response |
|---------|----------|
| Dominant voice | "Let's hear from others—Maria?" |
| Off-topic | "Great point—let's parking lot and circle back" |
| No decision | "What would help us decide today?" |
| Running late | "We have 5 min left—let's prioritize" |
| Tension | "Let's take a step back and restate the goal" |

---

## Step 5: Follow Up

**Goal:** Lock in value from the meeting.

### Prompt

```
Help me write a follow-up for this meeting.

What happened:
[Key discussions and outcomes]

Decisions made:
[What was decided]

Actions assigned:
[Who's doing what by when]

Open items:
[What's still unresolved]

Write a follow-up that:
1. Leads with decisions (not a narrative of the discussion)
2. Lists actions with owners and deadlines
3. Notes open items and next steps
4. Can be sent within 30 min of meeting end
5. Is short enough that people will actually read it

Format for email or Slack message.
```

### Output
- Ready-to-send follow-up
- Decisions captured
- Actions documented

---

## Meeting Types Quick Reference

| Meeting Type | Focus | Key Question |
|--------------|-------|--------------|
| Decision meeting | Making a choice | "What are we deciding today?" |
| Brainstorm | Generating ideas | "What options haven't we considered?" |
| Review | Evaluating work | "What feedback will make this better?" |
| Alignment | Getting on same page | "Are we all seeing this the same way?" |
| Update | Sharing information | "What do people need to know?" |

---

## Tips

- Cancel meetings that don't have a clear outcome
- Send agenda 24h+ ahead so people can prepare
- Follow up within 24 hours (ideally same day)
- If it can be an email, make it an email
- End 5 min early—people will love you
